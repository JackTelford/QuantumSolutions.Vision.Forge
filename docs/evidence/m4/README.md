## The three lane end to end

A fresh data directory, the reference fixture (`tests/plan/fixtures.ts`: s01 an 85 mm push-in on the bottle, s02 a turntable, both `three`; s03 the html title card), the obsidian look:

```
node scripts/cli.ts doctor --data-dir <data> --out <out>
node scripts/cli.ts assets --fetch hdri:studio_small_09-1k --data-dir <data> --out <out>
node scripts/cli.ts plan --brief brief.json --treatment treatment.json --shot-spec shot-spec.json --look look.json --data-dir <data> --out <out>
node scripts/cli.ts still --job <out>/cb8d8e66 --shot s01 --data-dir <data> --out <out>      (then s02, s03)
node scripts/cli.ts still --job <out>/cb8d8e66 --shot s01 --approve --data-dir <data> --out <out>   (then s02, s03)
node scripts/cli.ts render --job <out>/cb8d8e66 --data-dir <data> --out <out>
```

| Step | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `doctor` | `lanes { html: true, three: true, blender: true, audio: true }`, no problems |
| `assets --fetch` | `hdri:studio_small_09-1k` downloaded, 1615248 bytes, SHA-256 e7cfda5f… |
| `plan` | job `cb8d8e66`, no warnings (the fixture's lens now focuses on the bottle at 1.68 m; with the old 1.2 m `plan` prints the focus warning) |
| `still s01` | 7.1 s wall clock including the browser launch at 1920x1080; renderer hardware (chromium, d3d11); gate passed: composition 0 problems, no text, exposure YAVG 44.7 inside 24 to 140; frame 104 (the end of the push-in) |
| `still s02` | gate passed, exposure 44.2, frame 79 |
| `still s03` | gate passed (the html title card as in M3) |
| `render` | 315 frames in 39.7 s through one browser: s01 105 frames at 125.4 ms, s02 90 frames at 104.7 ms, s03 (html) 120 frames at 124.0 ms per 1080p frame including hashing; one frame per shot reused from its still |

The s01 frames show the bottle at the fixture's f/2.8: its front face is a few pixels soft against the focus plane at its axis (the thin-lens circle of confusion of 4 cm at 1.68 m is 0.4 percent of the frame), and the BokehPass leaves a thin bright halo along in-focus edges over the blurred wall (a known artefact of its fixed kernel, listed under Risks in the handoff).

## Calibration (ADR 0008 decision 2)

The gray card after tuning (`calibration.md`, scalars agx 2.57 and standard 3.25, keyed environment 0.05, lights white-balanced to the key):

| Render | Gray card YAVG (target 117.3 ± 5) | Mean RGB of the card | White (224.7 expected) | Black (49.5 expected) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| html, both tone mappings | 117.0 | 119, 119, 119 | 224.3 | 49.9 |
| three agx, studio HDRI | 116.6 | 117, 117, 117 | 173.0 (AgX rolls the highlight off) | 57.0 |
| three standard, studio HDRI | 117.2 | 118, 118, 118 | 211.0 | 61.2 |
| three agx, room environment | 119.8 | 121, 121, 121 | 176.1 | 59.3 |
| three standard, room environment | 120.2 | 121, 121, 121 | 216.8 | 62.4 |

The html lane's nine patches all sit within tolerance once the measurement converts RGB with the BT.709 matrix (before that fix the coloured swatches missed by up to 22 under ffmpeg's BT.601 default). The three lane's white and black are reported, not gated: the parity test (`tests/calibration/parity.integration.test.ts`, 10 tests in 13 s through Edge) holds its white between 150 and 235 and its black within −10/+20 and the two lanes' gray cards within 6 of each other.

Cost per frame: the calibration scene at 1920x1080 with four sub-frames and SSAA level 2 renders in 75 to 84 ms on the three lane, 49 ms on the html lane; at 480x270 (one sub-frame, SSAA level 1) 20 ms.
