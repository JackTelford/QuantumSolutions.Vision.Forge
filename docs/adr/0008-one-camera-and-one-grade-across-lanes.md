# ADR 0008: One physical camera model and one finishing grade across all lanes

Status: accepted, 2026-10-26; decisions 1, 2 and 3 amended 2026-10-29 with the three lane (M4).

## Context

A film reads as one piece when lens, light and grade rhyme across shots. The three lanes have different native conventions:

- Blender's camera uses a 36 mm sensor width with `sensor_fit = 'AUTO'` (36 mm on the larger image axis), `lens` in mm, `dof.aperture_fstop`, `dof.focus_distance`, and `render.motion_blur_shutter` in frames (0.5 = a 180-degree shutter).
- three.js `PerspectiveCamera` defaults to `filmGauge = 35` on the larger axis and a vertical `fov`; the same "50 mm" therefore frames differently unless `filmGauge` is set to 36. `BokehPass.aperture` is not an f-number.
- The html lane is a 2D stage; CSS `perspective` in pixels sets its vanishing geometry.
- AgX in the 3D lanes compresses saturated colors; the html lane is display-referred sRGB.

ffmpeg facts from probe P6: an identity 33³ `.cube` through `lut3d` with `format=gbrp16le` is lossless once the filter path is quoted correctly; seeded `noise` is reproducible run to run, but the grain on a frame processed alone differs from the same frame inside a sequence; RGB to `yuv420p` must name `out_color_matrix=bt709` or players shift hues; the round trip through libx264 CRF 16 measures 41.6 dB PSNR.

## Decision

1. `src/shared/camera/` bakes, per frame: position, rotation quaternion, focal length in mm on a 36 mm sensor (larger axis), f-stop, focus distance in meters, shutter angle in degrees, exposure offset in EV. Lane mappings: three sets `filmGauge = 36` and `setFocalLength(mm)`, derives `BokehPass` parameters by thin-lens math (`src/shared/camera/bokeh.ts`: the aperture factor is the circle of confusion per metre of defocus at the focus plane, capped at 1.5 percent of the frame) and renders motion blur by sub-frame accumulation across the shutter window, where each sub-frame's camera is interpolated from the baked frames (`src/shared/three/sub-frame.ts`: position, focus and focal length lerped, the quaternion slerped), never re-baked; Blender sets `sensor_width = 36`, `sensor_fit = 'AUTO'`, `lens`, `dof.*`, `motion_blur_shutter = angle / 360`; html sets `perspective` from the vertical field of view so 2.5D parallax matches.
2. Each look in the look bible carries a tone-mapping token (`agx` for photographic looks in both 3D lanes, `standard` for graphic looks) and brand-critical surfaces use an unlit "graphic" material path. The calibration scene is defined as data in `src/shared/calibration/scene.ts` (added 2026-10-29): the expected `YAVG` of a patch is computed on the gamma-encoded sRGB channels, Y' = 16 + 219 × (0.2126 R' + 0.7152 G' + 0.0722 B'), because `signalstats` reads the encoded luma plane of the delivery; the 18 percent gray card (`#767676`) therefore expects 117 ± 5, not the 55 a linear-light luma would give. Blender's world is Z-up, so the blender lane maps a baked world position (x, y, z) → (x, −z, y) before writing `scene.json`; because a camera, a light and a mesh keep the same local conventions in both worlds (local −Z forward, +Y up), an orientation maps as q_blender = q_M · q with q_M the +90-degree rotation about +X of that basis change, not by mapping the quaternion's vector part (corrected 2026-10-29 after the first Blender smoke render; `src/shared/camera/blender-axes.ts` holds both functions and the test that the identity camera on +Z becomes a Blender camera looking along +Y). A calibration scene (18 % gray card, white and black patches, six saturated swatches, glossy and matte spheres, 48 px text) is rendered by every lane and sets per-lane light-intensity scalars so the gray card's `YAVG` matches within ±5 and hue deltas are reported. Amended 2026-10-29 (M4): (a) a rig's relative light intensity is an irradiance on the subject, so the three lane gives a RectAreaLight the radiance intensity × scalar × d² / A and the blender lane must scale its watts by d² the same way, otherwise the fill (twice the key's size at a third of its intensity) outshines the key; (b) every lane balances its lights to the rig's key, each light's black-body colour divided channel by channel by the key's and scaled to a largest channel of 255 (`whiteBalancedKelvinRgb`), so a 5600 K key renders neutral and the lanes agree on neutrals, which the gray card's luma alone could not show (before the balance the three lane's gray card read 125, 115, 109); (c) the `standard` token maps to three's `LinearToneMapping` with a clamp (an identity curve), not `NoToneMapping`, so exposure still applies; (d) the white and black patches are reported, not gated, in the three lane: under AgX the 90 percent white rolls off to about 172 and the environment lifts the 2 percent black to 56 to 62, which are the tone curve's and the environment's doing, so the parity test holds the three lane's white between 150 and 235 and its black within −10/+20 of the expectation while the gray card stays within ±5 in both lanes and the two lanes' gray cards within 6 of each other.
3. `src/finishing/` turns look tokens into a 33³ `.cube` and one ffmpeg filter chain: `format=gbrp16le`, `lut3d` (tetrahedral), `vignette`, then `scale=out_color_matrix=bt709:out_range=tv` and a dithered `yuv420p` conversion with BT.709 tags; every luma measurement on a still (the gate's exposure and text-contrast checks, the calibration patches) converts RGB with the same BT.709 matrix (`scale=out_color_matrix=bt709` before `format=yuv420p`), since ffmpeg's default is BT.601 and a coloured box would otherwise read a luma no player shows (amended 2026-10-29). Grain (`noise` with an explicit seed) is applied only at assemble, never on stills, so the approved still can be compared with frame k of the render before grain. Amended 2026-10-29 (M7): the delivery grades per clip (`format=gbrp16le`, `lut3d`, then `format=rgb24` and `vignette` when the look has one) and lays the clip's text plate over the graded picture (`overlay=format=auto`), then joins the clips (cuts and xfade transitions; the grammar's dissolve is xfade's linear `fade`, never its noise `dissolve`), then grain, then the one BT.709 conversion; the join stays in 16-bit planar RGB unless the grade has a vignette or any clip carries a text plate, in which case every clip is pinned to 8-bit `gbrp` before the join (the old single chain already dropped to 8 bits for a vignette).
4. Picture plates are graded; text plates from the html lane are overlaid after the grade, and contrast and legibility checks run on the finished frame. Built 2026-10-29 (M7): a three or blender shot with text elements renders a second plate through the html lane with `plate: 'text'` (the text layers only, over a transparent root, captured as PNG with alpha) under its own key (the picture's key, the plate's name and the html lane's fingerprint), cached and resumable like a shot; the still gate composites it over the graded picture before measuring, `render` reports its frame pattern, and the assembly plan names it as the clip's overlay.
5. The `.cube` file hash and the tone-mapping token are part of the shot hash.

## Consequences

- "85 mm f/2 push-in" means the same framing and perspective in every lane, which is what makes the composition solver and the lighting cookbook lane-independent.
- Mixed-lane films (html type over three atmosphere over a Blender product) share one grade.
- Cross-lane exposure parity is measured, not asserted; the scalars live in the lighting cookbook and are re-run on tool upgrades.
